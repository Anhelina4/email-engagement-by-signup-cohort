# Email Engagement by Signup Cohort

Do new subscribers keep opening emails after signing up — and does that change over time?

## Data

1 Nov 2020 - 25 Feb 2021. Cohorts are weekly by registration date.

## Findings & Recommendations

- All cohorts behave similarly, maintaining engagement of 5.1-9.6% in the first 3 weeks after registration and dropping gradually from week 3 onwards, not significantly affected by changes in product or emails. 
- Only 6.8-9.4% of users open emails in their first week after registration. Reviewing the activation funnel could reveal what influences early engagement.
The exception is the cohort of 26 October 2020: registrations in the dataset only start on 1 Nov, so this week contains just 192 accounts and its numbers are noisier.
- The window to retain and engage users is the first 2-3 weeks after registration. After that, users are less interested in emails.
